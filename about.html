Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a love of baking and bringing neighbors together. Our neighborhood shop welcomes commuters, families, and anyone looking for a handmade treat to share.

Our Ingredients

We choose ingredients with quality and freshness in mind and seek local suppliers when available. Customers with allergies can contact our team to discuss ingredients before requesting a preorder.

Meet Our Team

Our bakers prepare breads, pastries, and cakes, while our counter team helps customers explore the selection and plan preorders. We share a commitment to careful preparation and friendly service.
